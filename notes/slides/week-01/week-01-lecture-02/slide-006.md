## Explanation

A **protocol** is a pre-existing agreement that allows communicating computers to understand one another.

## Walkthrough

The agreement specifies the **format** of messages, the **order** in which messages are exchanged, and the **actions** taken during transmission. A **message** is data sent over a network for computer interpretation.

The directions example makes the idea intuitive. One person asks a question, and the other replies using language and a sequence both people understand. If they do not share conventions about language, meaning, or turn-taking, communication breaks down. Network protocols provide those conventions for machines.

## Connections and exam relevance

Protocols are the rules that turn connected hardware into a functioning communication system. They operate alongside the hosts, applications, links, and forwarding devices introduced earlier. [week-01-lecture-02, p. 6]
