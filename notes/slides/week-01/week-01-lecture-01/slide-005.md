## Explanation

This slide states the course goals: understand what really happens when a user browses the web, and learn networking fundamentals that generalize across technologies.

## Walkthrough

The browsing questions preview the path we will study: how a request reaches a server, how a response returns, and which mechanisms support that exchange. The slide emphasizes fundamentals because ideas learned from wired Ethernet often carry over to Wi‑Fi, and Wi‑Fi fundamentals can help explain other wireless networks. This course focuses on Wi‑Fi and does not cover wireless sensor, mesh, or ad hoc networks in detail.

## Connections and exam relevance

These goals connect the earlier application examples to the mechanisms beneath them. They also explain why the course begins with general principles before specializing in particular technologies. [week-01-lecture-01, p. 5]
