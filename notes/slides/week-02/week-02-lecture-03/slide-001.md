## Explanation

The physical layer is concerned with representing bits as signals and carrying those signals across a communication channel, or link, from one network device to another. A channel may be wired, using cables, or wireless, using a medium such as radio without a cable. [week-02-lecture-03, p. 1]

## Walkthrough

The diagram shows a bit sequence such as `01010101` entering a channel, being represented as a physical signal, and being recovered as the same bit sequence at the other end. The channel has properties such as bandwidth and delay, which affect how quickly and reliably bits can be transmitted. [week-02-lecture-03, p. 1]

## Connections and exam relevance

This continues the previous lecture's distinction between the physical and data-link layers. The physical layer handles signals and the medium; the data-link layer interprets those bits as frames and handles local-link delivery. The physical layer is hop-by-hop: each link between neighboring devices has its own signal transmission. [week-02-lecture-03, p. 1]
